CPR AR MISSION

Interactive Training

📷 Marker: Place the HIRO marker at the predefined marked position beside the CPR manikin, then aim the camera at it.

🔊 Audio: Full CPR instructions are spoken for the same 13 steps. Audio can be muted.

🫀 Physical feedback: The original PRESTAN LEDs update automatically during Step 10. Press START COMPRESSIONS to begin your physical set and FINISH PHYSICAL SET when completed. Exact compression count and BPM are not automatically measured.

⏱️ Metronome: A pacing cue at 110/min (not a measured physical rate). The system does not measure compression depth, force, recoil, or hand placement.

👁️ Display control: The minimalist control can hide progress/step indicators.

Physical PRESTAN mode: During Step 10 press START COMPRESSIONS, perform 30 chest compressions on the manikin, then press FINISH PHYSICAL SET. The PRESTAN LEDs provide live monitor status; the system does not count individual compressions.
